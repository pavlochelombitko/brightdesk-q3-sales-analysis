# BrightDesk Q3 Sales Analysis

BrightDesk is a fictional retailer created for this portfolio project. I used synthetic sales data from ChatGPT to analyze Q3 revenue, profitability, and sales volume across four product categories and 20 products.

This project compares revenue, profit, and sales volume across four product categories and 20 individual products.

## Q3 at a glance

| Metric | Q3 total |
| --- | ---: |
| Sales transactions | 600 |
| Units sold | 2,199 |
| Revenue after discounts | €31,490.50 |
| Profit after discounts and product costs | €13,038.85 |

Profit in this report is revenue after discounts minus the cost of the units sold. The profit margin is profit divided by revenue after discounts.

## Category performance

| Category | Revenue after discounts | Profit | Units sold | Profit margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Electronics | €12,859.15 | €3,573.15 | 367 | 27.8% |
| Accessories | €7,056.75 | €3,188.75 | 274 | 45.2% |
| Home | €6,340.40 | €3,168.90 | 291 | 50.0% |
| Stationery | €5,234.20 | €3,108.05 | 1,267 | 59.4% |

### Revenue after discounts

Electronics generated the most revenue, about 146% more than Stationery, the lowest-revenue category.

![Revenue after discounts by category](pictures/Pasted%20image%2020261102205310.png)

### Profit after discounts and product costs

Electronics also led in total profit, but its lead over Stationery was just €465.10 (15.0%). Stationery retained €0.59 in profit per euro of revenue after discounts, compared with €0.28 for Electronics.

![Profit by category after discounts and product costs](pictures/Pasted%20image%2020261102210557.png)

### Units sold

Stationery sold 1,267 units, about 3.45 times as many as Electronics. Electronics earned €9.74 per unit in profit, compared with €2.45 for Stationery.

![Units sold by category](pictures/Pasted%20image%2020261102211507.png)

## Product performance

- **Notebook** led all products in both units sold (435) and profit (€1,854.80).
- **Backpack** ranked sixth by units sold (113) and second by profit (€1,686.25), earning €14.92 per unit.
- **Pencil** ranked fourth by units sold (174) and last by profit (€117.55), earning €0.68 per unit.

![Profit by product after discounts and product costs](pictures/Pasted%20image%2020261103221010.png)

![Units sold by product](pictures/Pasted%20image%2020261103221114.png)

## Recommended follow-up

Break down the Electronics–Stationery margin gap by product price, discount rate, unit cost, and sales mix. At the product level, compare high-volume, low-profit items such as Pencil with products such as Backpack to see where pricing, promotions, or sourcing warrant a closer look.
